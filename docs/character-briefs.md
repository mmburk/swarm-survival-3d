# LAST LIGHT — Character Design Briefs

Handoff document for an illustrator. Everything below describes characters that
already exist in the game as vector portraits; this brief is for someone
re-drawing them properly, at higher fidelity, in the same art direction.

---

## 1. The world, in one paragraph

`LAST LIGHT` is a top-down roguelite set in a dead American town some months
after an outbreak. It is not sci-fi and it is not stylised horror — it is a
place where the power went out and never came back. **Nothing in this world
glows on purpose.** Light comes from petrol lamps, road flares, muzzle flash and
a grey overcast sky. Every colour is desaturated. If a colour looks like it
belongs on a synthwave poster, it does not belong here.

The player walks out of a shelter each night, fights through several city
sectors, and either walks back or does not. Five survivors are still
broadcasting on the radio, and each of them helps the player in the only way
they know how. The structure is borrowed from *Hades*: the five voices on the
band are this game's pantheon.

## 2. Art direction

The target is **Hades-style painted key art**: bold, graphic, confident, built
from flat colour and heavy ink rather than rendering. Specifically:

- **Three-quarter turned head.** Never a symmetrical front-on mugshot. The nose
  and lips break the silhouette on the near side; the far eye is crowded against
  the profile edge and foreshortened; the ear sits about two thirds back and the
  jaw line runs forward from under it to the chin.
- **One hard-edged shadow.** A single straight-edged shadow wedge cuts
  diagonally across the face. Two tones plus a deep occlusion tone — no soft
  gradients, no ambient occlusion mush. The key light is front-left.
- **A back light behind the figure.** A thick warm rim stroke tracing the
  *outermost* silhouette — the cap, the hood, the hair — brightest where the
  surface turns hardest into the lamp, faded out by the shoulder. It must not
  read as an outline drawn around the whole head.
- **Angular, faceted shapes.** Hair, cloth and headgear are knife-edged shards,
  not rounded caps. Straight brows, not arcs.
- **Heavy, varied ink.** Thick contour on the silhouette, lighter interior
  lines, plus a few loose unclosed accent strokes left visible, the way a
  painter's sketch lines survive into the final.
- **Cropped tight.** The bust overflows the frame at the shoulders.

Backdrop for each portrait: a bruise of the character's signature colour behind
the head, two diagonal slashes of the same colour, soot everywhere else, and a
film of paper grain over the top. Portrait aspect ratio **5:6**.

## 3. THE PLAYER — "the one carrying the gun"

**Never named. Never speaks. Face never fully shown.**

The most important character and the hardest one: the player must be iconic in
silhouette but anonymous in identity. The person under the hood could be
anyone still walking, and that is the point.

- **Silhouette:** hood up, angular and faceted, cutting a hard oblique arch
  around the face. Head turned three-quarters like everyone else, so the hood
  opening is an asymmetric arch, not a symmetrical hole.
- **Face:** only the eyes and the bridge above them are visible. The hood
  throws a hard-edged shadow band straight across the brow; the eyes catch the
  key light beneath it. Slightly narrowed, set, tired but not defeated. Not
  heroic, not grim-dark — the face of somebody who has done this walk before
  and expects to do it again.
- **Scarf:** rust-red, pulled up over the mouth and chin, its top edge cutting
  just under the nose so the nose still breaks the profile. It must read as
  **wrapped cloth**, not a surgical mask: one diagonal fold crossing the face,
  straight creases rather than parallel pleats, and a visible knot on the far
  side with a loose tail thrown back off the shoulder.
- **Body:** olive field jacket, a scrap steel plate strapped over the chest
  (bolted, scratched, the only armour in the game), a canvas pack with a
  bedroll, and a sling strap crossing the torso.
- **Palette:** skin `#c49669` lit / `#7f563a` shadow / `#472e1e` core;
  jacket and hood `#5a5236` / `#332f1e`; scarf `#7a3b2c` with `#552518` in
  shadow and `#a05c44` on the lit folds; chest plate `#4b4f45`;
  signature colour rust `#a8552b`; rim light `#ffbe72`.
- **Symbol:** a lit window in a dead building — a simple house-gable outline
  with one glowing pane, and a cross-bar of window frame. It means the last
  light still on in the town.

## 4. SARGE

> *121.5 — GUARD FREQ* · "Aim small, miss small. Out."

National Guard sergeant who never stood down. Talks in clipped instructions.
Gives the player raw lethality — fire rate, damage, faster heavy attacks.

- **Build:** heavy square jaw, thick neck, mid-forties, weather-beaten.
- **Head:** olive patrol cap, hard-faceted crown, brim jammed low and throwing
  a real shadow across the eyes. A rust-coloured vertical tab stitched up the
  front of the crown.
- **Face:** deep-set eyes under a permanent scowl, straight heavy brows, flat
  stubble laid on as one shape along the jaw rather than drawn as hairs.
  A vertical scar with two cross-stitch marks running down the far cheek —
  he does not talk about it.
- **Body:** olive fatigues, V-neck collar, three rust rank chevrons stencilled
  on the shoulder at a slight angle.
- **Expression:** mouth set in a flat line. He is not pleased to see you; he is
  simply still on duty.
- **Palette:** skin `#c08f61` / `#7c5335` / `#4a2f1c`; fatigues `#4b5136` /
  `#2b301d`; hair `#2e2820`; signature rust `#a8552b`; rim `#ffb968`.
- **Symbol:** three sergeant's chevrons, nothing else.

## 5. DR. VASQUEZ

> *148.2 — CDC RELAY* · "It is a pathogen. Use it back on them."

Field epidemiologist still running a relay from a collapsed CDC post. Gives the
player the infection turned around — corpses that rupture, rounds that carry
through bodies, kills that return health.

- **Build:** lean, sharp features, exhausted. Late thirties.
- **Head:** black hair pulled back hard and tied off in a wedge at the back,
  with a fringe of jagged shards over the forehead. Rubber goggles shoved up
  onto the forehead — two rectangular lenses, one pale and catching light, one
  in shadow, on a dark strap.
- **Face:** the most tired face in the cast. Heavy lids, a faint line under
  each eye, brows angled up at the inner ends — worry, not anger.
- **Body:** moss-green hazmat suit, collar unsealed, a chest zip, and a small
  green medical roundel with a cross on the shoulder. A grey respirator hangs
  unhooked under her jaw on two straps — she took it off to talk and never put
  it back on.
- **Expression:** flat, level, past being frightened.
- **Palette:** skin `#bd8a63` / `#7a5138` / `#452c1e`; suit `#7d8a5c` /
  `#414a2c`; hair `#1d1815`; signature moss `#6f8a3c`; rim `#d9f08a`.
- **Symbol:** a specimen vial with something settled in the bottom of it, and
  two bubbles rising.

## 6. MAE

> *27.185 — CB CH.19* · "If it jams, hit it. If it still jams, hit it harder."

A mechanic on a CB radio, the youngest voice on the band and the only one
enjoying any of this. Gives the player upgrades to the gun itself — velocity,
penetration, pickup range, rate of fire.

- **Build:** young, wiry, mid-twenties.
- **Head:** ball cap worn backwards — crown faceted, the small tab of the strap
  visible at the front. Welding goggles parked on top of the cap: two amber-brown
  lenses joined by a bridge. Copper-brown hair escaping in hard shards below the
  cap on both sides.
- **Face:** the only genuine smile in the game — an open asymmetric grin, teeth
  suggested by one dark shape rather than drawn. Freckles across the nose and
  near cheek. One honest smear of engine oil on the far cheek.
- **Body:** blue mechanic's coveralls, V-collar, and a CB handset clipped to the
  shoulder strap with amber detailing and a stub antenna.
- **Expression:** delighted. She has an excuse to use every tool she owns.
- **Palette:** skin `#d2a374` / `#8d6440` / `#513722`; coveralls `#42506a` /
  `#242c3c`; hair `#8a4f1e` with `#c07c33` highlights; signature amber
  `#c99a3c`; rim `#ffd07a`.
- **Symbol:** a single open-ended wrench, tilted.

## 7. PASTOR ELI

> *89.9 — CHURCH FM* · "You are not finished yet, son."

An old minister broadcasting from a church transmitter. Gives the player
survival — maximum health, damage reduction, and extra chances to get back up.

- **Build:** gaunt, long-jawed, seventies. The oldest and frailest silhouette
  in the cast, and the calmest.
- **Head:** balding — a band of grey hair around the temples and back with the
  crown bare. Two straight cut lines across the forehead for age.
- **Beard:** the shape that *is* him. A heavy angular grey-white wedge hanging
  well below the jaw, with a lighter core catching the key light and a darker
  facet on the shadow side, plus a separate moustache slab over the mouth line.
  **Important:** the beard must not climb to the temples and meet the hair, or
  the head closes into a ring and he reads as a nun rather than a bearded man.
  Leave a clear gap at the ear.
- **Face:** deep-set eyes, lids low, brows angled up at the inner ends. Skin
  weathered a shade darker than the rest of the cast on purpose — pale beard
  over pale skin under a white rim light turns the head into one blank mass.
- **Body:** black clerical shirt with a white collar tab, and a small pale cross
  on a cord at the chest.
- **Expression:** patient. He is not asking anything of you.
- **Palette:** skin `#a9805c` / `#6d4d34` / `#3f2a1b`; shirt `#24231d` /
  `#111008`; hair `#8b8574`; beard `#6a6455` with `#4a4639` in shadow;
  signature bone `#c6c2b0`; rim `#e8dcc0`.
- **Symbol:** a cross made of two nailed boards, with the nail head visible.

## 8. THE UNKNOWN (internally "QUIET")

> *??? — CARRIER ONLY* · *[the signal cuts out]*

There is a sixth signal that is only a carrier wave. It offers the sharpest
upgrades in the game and every one of them costs something — crit chance and
crit damage in exchange for maximum health, movement speed for durability. The
player never learns who or what this is, and the design must never answer it.

- **Silhouette:** a deep hood, long and angular, worn by something roughly
  person-shaped. Turned three-quarters like the rest of the cast so the hood
  opening is an oblique arch. No visible hands, no visible body.
- **Inside the hood:** black. Not dark — black. Two horizontal slits of dull red
  light where eyes would be, the near one brighter with a warm pale core, the
  far one dimmer and shorter. Nothing else. No face, no mouth, no jaw.
- **Overlay:** three faint horizontal interference lines across the whole
  portrait, and along the bottom a red carrier waveform that runs flat and then
  breaks.
- **Expression:** none, and that is the design.
- **Palette:** cloth `#2c261c` / `#14100a`; interior `#080705`; signature blood
  `#8e2323`; rim `#e03a2c` — the only cold-blooded rim in the cast; everyone
  else is lit warm.
- **Symbol:** a carrier wave that spikes once and then stops mid-sentence,
  followed by two fading dots, inside a thin circle.

## 9. Deliverables per character

1. **Bust portrait**, 5:6, three-quarter, on the character's backdrop. Used on
   the boon-offer cards and the pin board at roughly 130–240px wide, so it must
   survive being read small.
2. **One-colour symbol**, square, silhouette only, no interior detail, legible
   down to **16px**. It is nailed over doorways and stamped on tokens, and at
   that size a face is mud but a shape the player has seen five times is not.
3. Optional: a **struck-plate variant** of the symbol — the mark stamped into an
   octagonal salvage plate with four rivets and a bevelled inner face, tinted in
   the character's signature colour, matching the game's currency tokens.
